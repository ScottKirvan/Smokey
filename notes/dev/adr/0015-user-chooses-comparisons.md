# ADR-0015: The user chooses what to compare; no peer discovery

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-10-09

## Context

Comparable developers are hard to find; comparable and model repos are easier. Two comparison jobs came up: modelling a repo on another, and head-to-head comparison of developers (for example, to understand what makes a Sponsors page work).

## Decision

- Comparison covers repo against repo and account against account.
- The user always picks who or what to compare. Finding comparable accounts or repos is out of scope.
- Org against org can wait.

## Consequences

- No search or recommendation features; comparison takes explicit subjects.
